Features

Everything you need to add crisp, performant Fog of War to your game.

🔮

Pixel-Perfect Rendering

Per-pixel screen-space fog with no resolution limits. Stays razor-sharp at any world size, with zero grid artifacts.

👁

Raycast Line of Sight

Burst-accelerated raycasting computes true line of sight with accurate edge detection, corners, and occlusion.

🎨

Deep Customization

Solid, grayscale, blur, texture, and outline fog styles. Pixelate, dither, soften, invert, and tint to taste.

🧘

Hiders

Hide units and objects outside line of sight. Built-in behaviors, custom hooks, and per-revealer visibility events.

⚡

Built for Scale

Spatial acceleration, time-sliced updates, static revealers, and staged GPU uploads keep thousands of revealers fast.

🗺

Minimap & Memory

Generate a minimap texture, regrow explored fog over time, and save/load exploration state to disk.
