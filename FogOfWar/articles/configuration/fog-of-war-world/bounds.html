Bounds

The FOW Bounds group of the Fog Of War World inspector. This section appears whenever bounds are required — when Darken Out-Of-World-Bounds Area? is on (see Rendering Options), when using Minimap Generation, or when Fog Sample Mode is Texture Storage.

Property Type Description
Center Vector3 Center of the world bounds volume. Code: WorldBounds.center.
Extents Vector3 Extents of the world bounds volume. Code: WorldBounds.extents.

You can also drag the box gizmo in the Scene view to set the bounds, or update them at runtime with UpdateWorldBounds.

Warning

If the bounds are left at their default size, the inspector warns "Make sure to actually set your world bounds!"
