Unity 2021.3+ Built-In URP HDRP Any Shader Editor Tool

Features

Everything you need to make flat, papery foliage read as solid, well-lit volumes.

🌳

Canopy Normal Transfer

Auto-generates a smooth proxy mesh around your foliage and transfers its normals onto the source mesh, so leaves catch light like a single solid volume.

🌿

One-Click Grass Mode

Super fast, super simple normals for grass, clovers, and small details. Enable the tickbox, click generate, done.

☀

Vertex Color AO Bake

Bake high-quality ambient occlusion into any vertex color channel (R/G/B/A) using the generated proxy mesh.

🎨

Works With Any Shader

No included shader required. Compatible with Built-In, URP, HDRP, and third-party systems like TVE out of the box.

🔄

Non-Destructive

Your original mesh is always preserved. Restore it at any time with a single click — nothing is baked into your build.

🧮

LOD & Multi-Part Support

Proxy Stealer components share one proxy across LOD levels and separated trunk/leaf meshes for consistent results.
