The Workflow at a Glance

  1. First Steps — Add the Foliage Renormalizer Utility component, set your target renderer, save paths, and submesh selection.
  2. Choose your mode:
    • Grass Mode for grass and small details.
    • General Mode for trees, bushes, and larger plants.
  3. Generate your new mesh, then handle double-sided rendering on your material if needed.
